DATA EXPLORATION & MODELLING

Bike Sharing
Demand Analysis

What drives hourly bike rentals in Washington, DC? Using two years of Capital Bikeshare data, I found commuter peaks at 8 AM and 5–6 PM and a moderate link with temperature (r = 0.40). I then compared linear, Poisson and negative binomial regression on a held-out later period. All three beat the baseline, with Poisson cutting MAE by about 39%. The diagnostics showed strong autocorrelation the models missed.

BUSINESS & DATA ANALYSIS

E-commerce
SQL Analysis

About 100,000 Olist orders from Brazilian online retailers, analysed in PostgreSQL and summarised in a three-page Power BI dashboard. I checked data quality, then measured sales, customers, products and delivery. It showed 13.2M in delivered product revenue, about 97% of customers ordering only once, and an 8.1% late-delivery rate concentrated in the north-east.
